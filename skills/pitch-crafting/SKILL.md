# Pitch Crafting Skill

## Principles of Non-AI Sounding Copy
* **Zero Fluff**: Never begin with *"I hope you are doing well"*, *"Allow me to introduce myself"*, or *"In the ever-evolving landscape"*.
* **Hyper-Specific Hook**: The first sentence must reference a verifiable milestone or observation specific to the prospect's company.
* **Bridge**: Connect their specific challenge directly to the outcome you deliver in 1 sentence.
* **Low-Friction Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Avoid *"Let's book a 30-minute demo"*. Instead, ask an interest-based question like *"Worth a brief look?"* or *"Open to seeing a 2-minute workflow breakdown?"*.
* **Length**: Under 85 words total.
